# ✈️ AIRLINE FLIGHT PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

## 📊 Tableau Business Intelligence Dashboard

### 📝 1. Project Overview

This project presents an *interactive Airline Flight Performance Dashboard developed using Tableau* to provide management with a clear and consolidated view of airline operations.

The dashboard focuses on *flight volume, departure delays, arrival delays and flight cancellations*, enabling management to monitor operational performance, identify inefficiencies and support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard transforms raw flight data into meaningful *KPIs, trends and comparative visualizations* for quick business understanding.

---

## 🎯 2. Project Objectives

The key objectives of this analysis are:

* ✈️ *Monitor overall flight operations* and total flight volume.
* ⏱️ *Measure departure and arrival delays* across airlines.
* ❌ *Analyze flight cancellations* and identify major cancellation reasons.
* 📊 *Compare airline performance* based on flight activity and delays.
* 📅 *Identify monthly flight trends* and operational patterns.
* 💡 Provide management with *actionable insights for operational improvement*.

# 📂 4. Dataset Overview

The dataset contains flight-level information that can be used to evaluate airline operational performance.

The analysis focuses primarily on:

* ✈️ Airline
* 📅 Month
* ⏱️ Departure Delay
* 🛬 Arrival Delay
* ❌ Cancellation Status
* 📋 Cancellation Reason
* 📊 Total Flight Count

These fields were transformed into meaningful business metrics and visualizations within Tableau.

---

# 🧹 5. Data Preparation

Before developing the dashboard, the dataset was prepared for analysis through the following process:

### 🔹 Data Cleaning

* Removed unnecessary or irrelevant records.
* Checked for missing and inconsistent values.
* Verified data types for numerical and categorical fields.
* Standardized airline and cancellation-related fields.

### 🔹 Data Preparation

* Created required aggregations for flight counts.
* Calculated average departure delay.
* Calculated average arrival delay.
* Categorized cancellation reasons.
* Prepared monthly flight-level analysis.

### 🔹 Data Validation

The prepared data was reviewed to ensure that the dashboard KPIs and visualizations represented the underlying dataset accurately.

# 📌 6. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides four primary KPIs:

### ✈️ Total Flights — *7,432*

Represents the overall number of flights covered in the analysis.

*Management Perspective:*
This KPI provides a high-level understanding of the operational volume handled across the airlines.

---

### ⏱️ Average Departure Delay — *9.55*

Represents the average delay experienced at departure.

*Management Perspective:*
This metric helps identify potential operational issues related to scheduling, aircraft turnaround, boarding and airport processes.

---

### 🛬 Average Arrival Delay — *4.41*

Represents the average delay recorded when flights arrive at their destination.

*Management Perspective:*
A lower arrival delay indicates better schedule adherence and operational efficiency.

---

### ❌ Cancelled Flights — *116*

Represents the total number of cancelled flights in the analyzed dataset.

*Management Perspective:*
Monitoring cancellations helps management identify operational disruptions and areas requiring corrective action.
